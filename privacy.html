Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

This policy explains what data Ticker Press Publisher ("the app") handles. The app is operated by Abel Prima Muñoz ("the operator"), based in Spain, who is also the only person who uses it.

What the app does

The app uploads the operator's own videos to the operator's own TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API. Only one TikTok account is connected: the operator's.

Data the app handles

  • The TikTok open ID and display name of the connected account.
  • The access token and refresh token that TikTok issues after the operator signs in, which the app needs to upload videos.
  • The status of each upload (for example "processing" or "published").
  • The video files and captions that the operator has produced.

The app does not collect personal data about any other person, including viewers, followers or commenters. It has no accounts, forms, cookies or analytics, and this website sets no cookies.

How the data is used

Only to upload the operator's videos to the operator's account and to confirm the result. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Where it is stored

Tokens are kept as encrypted secrets in a private code repository's automation settings. They are not published or embedded in any public code.

Sharing

The operator does not sell or share this data with third parties. Videos and captions are sent to TikTok, and to Instagram, as part of publishing. These platforms handle them under their own policies.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the connection is active. The operator can end it at any time by deleting the stored tokens and by removing the app's access in TikTok's account settings under connected apps.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you can ask what data is held about you, or ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Since the app holds data only about its operator, such requests are expected to be rare, but you can write to tickerpressyt@gmail.com and will receive a reply.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly handle data about them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
