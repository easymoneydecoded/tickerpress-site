Ticker Press Publisher

A personal publishing tool for the Ticker Press finance channel.

Ticker Press is an English-language YouTube channel that publishes short educational videos about markets and personal finance. Ticker Press Publisher is the small program that sends those same videos to the channel owner's own TikTok account once they are ready.

What it does

  • Takes the short videos the owner has already produced and published on YouTube.
  • Uploads them to a single TikTok account: the owner's, which the owner has authorized through TikTok Login.
  • Reports back whether each upload succeeded.

What it does not do

  • It is not a public service and has no sign-up. Only the owner's account is connected.
  • It does not read, collect or store any information about TikTok viewers or other users.
  • It does not add logos, watermarks or links to the videos it uploads.
Videos are produced with AI tools (synthetic voice and stock footage) and are labelled as AI-generated content where the platform provides that option. They are for education and are not financial advice.
